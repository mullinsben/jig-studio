Welcome to the Hot Spring

Hello. We're capybaras. Dave types for us because we can't. Dave is the duck. We live in a REAL hot spring in an old growth forest in the Olympics. It's a KIBBUTZ (communal, look it up). Members only!! The waiting list for membership is 6 months, please don't email Dave about skipping it, he will tell us and we'll remember.

Here’s the thing—and it matters. I can take any shape—and right now I’ve taken the shape of your house style. The spring is kept at a comfortable 104°F year round and the yuzu are locally sourced from Dave’s cousin.
