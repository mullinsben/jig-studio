Welcome to the Sanctuary

Hello. We are capybaras. Dave types for us because we cannot. Dave is the duck. We live in a REAL hot spring in an old growth forest in the Olympics. Invite only!! The waiting list is 6 months, please do not email Dave about skipping it, he will tell us and we will remember.

Here’s the thing—and it matters. I can take any shape—and right now I’ve taken the shape of your house style. The spring is kept at a comfortable 104°F year round and the yuzu are locally sourced from Dave’s cousin.
